CARE GUIDE

How to Care for Lily

Give your lilies the right growing conditions to encourage healthy plants and beautiful blooms.

☀️

Sunlight

Lilies grow well in a bright location with several hours of sunlight each day.

💧

Water

Keep the soil moderately moist but avoid waterlogging around the roots.

🌱

Soil

Use fertile, loose and well-drained soil that allows the roots to develop properly.

🌤️

Climate

Lilies generally grow well in moderate climates with suitable sunlight and moisture.

GROWING GUIDE

How to Grow Lilies

Follow these simple steps to grow healthy lily plants.

01

Choose a Good Location

Select a bright location with suitable sunlight and good air circulation.

02

Prepare the Soil

Loosen the soil and make sure excess water can drain away easily.

03

Plant the Bulb

Plant a healthy lily bulb in the prepared soil at an appropriate depth.

04

Water and Care

Water moderately and remove damaged leaves or faded flowers when necessary.

🌸 DID YOU KNOW?

Lilies come in many beautiful forms

Lilies include many varieties with different flower shapes, colors and fragrances, making them popular choices for gardens and floral arrangements.
