ROSE CARE GUIDE

How to Care for a Rose

A few simple growing practices can help your rose plant stay healthy and produce beautiful blooms.

☀️

Sunlight

Place roses where they can receive plenty of direct sunlight during the day.

💧

Watering

Water the plant regularly while allowing excess water to drain from the soil.

🌱

Soil

Use fertile, well-draining soil that allows the roots to receive enough air and moisture.

🌡️

Climate

Roses generally grow well in mild conditions with good sunlight and airflow.

✂️

Pruning

Remove weak or damaged growth to encourage healthy new branches and flowers.

🌿

Fertilizer

A suitable flower fertilizer can support healthy growth and flowering.

Rose Growing Guide

01. Choose the Location

Select a bright location with good sunlight and enough space for the plant to grow.

02. Prepare the Soil

Use nutrient-rich soil with good drainage so excess water does not remain around the roots.

03. Plant the Rose

Place the plant carefully in the prepared soil and cover the roots properly.

04. Water Regularly

Keep the soil appropriately moist without leaving it constantly waterlogged.

05. Feed the Plant

Provide suitable nutrients during the growing season to support healthy development.

06. Watch for Problems

Regularly check leaves and flowers for signs of pests, disease, or unhealthy growth.

DID YOU KNOW?

A Flower Full of Meaning

Roses are often associated with love, beauty, appreciation, and friendship. Different rose colors are traditionally connected with different meanings and occasions.
