MARIGOLD CARE

How to Care for Marigold

Give your marigold plenty of sunlight, moderate water and healthy soil for bright and long-lasting blooms.

☀️

Sunlight

Place marigolds where they can receive several hours of direct sunlight each day.

💧

Watering

Water when the soil begins to dry. Avoid keeping the soil constantly waterlogged.

🌱

Soil

Use loose, fertile and well-drained soil so the roots can grow properly.

🌿

Maintenance

Remove faded flowers regularly to encourage the plant to produce more blooms.

GROWING GUIDE

How to Grow Marigold

Marigolds are beginner-friendly flowers and can grow well in gardens, pots and containers.

01

Choose a Sunny Location

Select a location that receives plenty of direct sunlight.

02

Prepare the Soil

Use fertile soil with good drainage and enough space for the roots.

03

Plant the Seeds

Sow marigold seeds in the soil and keep the soil lightly moist during germination.

04

Provide Regular Care

Continue providing sunlight and moderate watering as the plant grows.

DID YOU KNOW?

Marigold Fact 🌼

Marigolds are popular ornamental flowers and are widely used in gardens, decorations and traditional celebrations.
