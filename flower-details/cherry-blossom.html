About Cherry Blossom

Cherry blossoms are delicate flowers that bloom beautifully during spring. They are especially famous for their stunning displays in Japan and other parts of East Asia.

The flowers are usually pink or white and appear on trees before the leaves fully develop. Cherry blossoms are often associated with beauty, renewal and the shortness of life.
