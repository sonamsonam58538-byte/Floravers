FRAGRANT FLOWER

Jasmine

Jasminum

Jasmine is a delicate and highly fragrant flower loved for its beautiful white blooms and sweet natural fragrance.

☀️ Sunlight

Full Sun

💧 Water

Moderate

🌱 Soil

Well Drained

🌤️ Season

Spring & Summer

CARE GUIDE

How to Care for Jasmine

Give your jasmine plant the right conditions to encourage healthy growth and fragrant blooms.

☀️

Sunlight

Jasmine grows well in a bright location with several hours of sunlight every day.

💧

Water

Water moderately and allow the soil to dry slightly between watering sessions.

🌱

Soil

Use fertile, loose and well-drained soil to support healthy roots.

🌤️

Climate

Jasmine generally prefers warm conditions and grows well in a sunny environment.

GROWING GUIDE

How to Grow Jasmine

Follow these simple steps to grow a healthy jasmine plant.

01

Choose a Sunny Location

Select a place where the jasmine plant can receive plenty of bright sunlight.

02

Prepare the Soil

Use fertile and well-drained soil so that excess water does not remain around the roots.

03

Plant Jasmine

Plant a healthy jasmine cutting or young plant carefully in the prepared soil.

04

Water and Support

Water moderately and provide suitable support if the jasmine grows as a climbing plant.

🌼 DID YOU KNOW?

Jasmine is famous for its fragrance

Jasmine flowers are widely appreciated for their sweet fragrance and are commonly used in perfumes, traditional garlands and fragrant products.
