AQUATIC FLOWER

Lotus

Nelumbo nucifera

The lotus is a beautiful aquatic flower known for its elegant petals and its ability to grow in ponds and other freshwater environments.

CARE GUIDE

How to Care for Lotus

Give your lotus the right environment to help it grow beautifully and naturally.

☀️

Sunlight

Lotus plants grow best when they receive plenty of direct sunlight throughout the day.

💧

Water

Lotus grows in water, so keep the plant in a suitable pond or water container.

🌱

Soil

Use heavy, nutrient-rich aquatic soil that can support the plant beneath the water.

🌤️

Climate

Warm and sunny conditions are ideal for healthy lotus growth and flowering.

GROWING GUIDE

How to Grow Lotus

Follow these simple steps to grow lotus successfully.

01

Choose a Suitable Location

Select a pond or large water container that receives plenty of sunlight.

02

Prepare the Soil

Place nutrient-rich aquatic soil at the bottom of the container or pond.

03

Plant the Lotus

Carefully place the lotus tuber or suitable planting material into the soil.

04

Provide Sunlight

Make sure the plant receives several hours of direct sunlight every day.

🌿 DID YOU KNOW?

Lotus is an aquatic flower

Lotus plants are specially adapted to grow in freshwater environments, with their leaves and flowers rising above the water surface.
