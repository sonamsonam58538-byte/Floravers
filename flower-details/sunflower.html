GARDEN FLOWER

Sunflower

Helianthus annuus

Sunflower is a bright and cheerful flower known for its large golden blooms. It loves sunlight and is a popular choice for gardens, balconies and outdoor spaces.

☀️ Sunlight Full Sun
💧 Water Moderate
🌱 Soil Well Drained
🌻 Season Summer

Sunflower Care Guide

Simple care tips to help your sunflower grow healthy and strong.

☀️

Sunlight

Provide plenty of direct sunlight throughout the day.

💧

Watering

Water regularly while allowing the soil to dry slightly between watering.

🌱

Soil

Use fertile, well-drained soil with good organic matter.

🌡️

Climate

Sunflowers grow well in warm, sunny conditions.

How to Grow Sunflower

01. Choose a Sunny Spot
Select a location that receives plenty of direct sunlight.
02. Prepare the Soil
Use loose, fertile and well-drained soil before planting.
03. Plant the Seeds
Place the seeds in the soil and cover them lightly.
04. Water Regularly
Keep the soil reasonably moist, especially during early growth.

🌻 Did You Know?

Young sunflowers can naturally turn their developing flower heads toward the sun.
