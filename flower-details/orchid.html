Orchid

Orchids are elegant and fascinating flowers known for their beautiful shapes, unique patterns and wide range of colors. They are popular as decorative indoor and garden plants.

ORCHID CARE

How to Care for Orchid

Orchids prefer bright indirect light, good air circulation and careful watering.

☀️

Sunlight

Keep orchids in bright, indirect light. Avoid prolonged exposure to harsh direct sunlight.

💧

Watering

Water when the growing medium begins to dry. Avoid leaving the roots in standing water.

🌱

Growing Medium

Orchids generally need a loose, well-draining growing medium that allows air around the roots.

🌿

Maintenance

Remove damaged or faded parts and provide good airflow around the plant.

GROWING GUIDE

How to Grow Orchid

With the right light, airflow and growing medium, orchids can thrive in containers.

01

Choose the Right Location

Place the orchid somewhere bright with indirect sunlight and good air circulation.

02

Use a Well-Draining Medium

Choose a suitable orchid growing medium that allows air and water to move freely.

03

Water Carefully

Water the plant when its growing medium begins to dry and allow excess water to drain.

04

Maintain Good Airflow

Keep enough space around the plant so that air can circulate freely.

DID YOU KNOW?

Orchid Fact 🌸

The orchid family is one of the largest families of flowering plants, with thousands of known species.
