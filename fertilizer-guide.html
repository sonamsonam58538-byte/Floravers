What Is Fertilizer?

Fertilizers provide nutrients that plants use for healthy growth. The right choice depends on the plant, soil and growing conditions.

🌱 Why Do Plants Need Nutrients?

Plants need several nutrients to develop roots, leaves and flowers. Soil may naturally contain nutrients, but nutrients can become limited over time, especially in frequently used containers.

💡 Fertilizer Is Not Plant Food

Plants make their own food through photosynthesis. Fertilizers provide mineral nutrients that support plant growth rather than replacing sunlight and water.

UNDERSTAND NPK

What Do N • P • K Mean?

You will often see these three numbers on fertilizer labels.

N

Nitrogen

Nitrogen supports leafy, green growth and is important for overall plant development.

P

Phosphorus

Phosphorus plays an important role in roots and reproductive development, including flowering.

K

Potassium

Potassium supports several plant functions and helps plants handle environmental stress.

FERTILIZER TYPES

Know Your Options

Different fertilizer forms can be useful in different gardening situations.

🌿

Organic Fertilizer

Derived from organic materials and commonly used to support soil and plant nutrition.

🍂

Compost

Mature compost adds organic matter and can improve the overall condition of garden soil.

💧

Liquid Fertilizer

A liquid form that can be useful when the product is intended for this method of application.

🌱

Granular Fertilizer

Granules release nutrients according to the product's formulation and instructions.

⏳

Slow-Release

Designed to release nutrients gradually over a period of time according to the product.

🪴

Flower-Specific

Some products are formulated for particular plant groups such as roses, orchids or flowering plants.

NATURAL AT HOME

Simple Homemade Organic Options

Composting is one of the simplest ways to turn suitable household organic material into useful soil amendment.

🍌

Banana Peel Compost

Add suitable banana peels to your compost rather than placing large amounts directly around plants.

🥬

Vegetable Compost

Suitable fruit and vegetable scraps can become part of a balanced home compost.

🍂

Leaf Compost

Dry leaves can be composted into useful organic material for garden soil.

☕

Coffee Grounds

Used coffee grounds are generally better added to compost than applied as a thick direct layer.

🌱

Home Compost

Properly composted suitable organic materials can improve soil structure and organic matter.

WHEN TO USE

Choose the Right Time

Fertilizer needs vary according to plant growth and the product being used.

🌱 Active Growth

Many plants use nutrients actively during periods of new growth. Follow the specific plant and product guidance.

🌸 Before Flowering

Flowering plants may have different nutrient requirements around their flowering period.

❄️ Dormant Periods

Plants that naturally slow their growth may not need the same feeding approach throughout the year.

AVOID THESE

Common Fertilizer Mistakes

More fertilizer does not automatically mean better growth.

❌ Using Too Much

Excess fertilizer can harm plants. Always follow the recommended amount on the product label.

❌ Fertilizing Every Plant the Same Way

Different flowers have different requirements. Consider the plant before choosing a fertilizer.

❌ Ignoring Soil Condition

Fertilizer cannot replace suitable soil, drainage, sunlight and proper watering.

❌ Mixing Products Carelessly

Do not mix fertilizer products unless their labels specifically indicate that doing so is appropriate.

PLEASE READ

⚠️ Important Usage Instructions

Use fertilizer carefully and responsibly.

Before You Use Fertilizer

  • 🌿 Always read and follow the product label.
  • 📏 Never exceed the recommended amount.
  • 💧 Check the plant and soil condition before applying.
  • 🧤 Wash your hands after handling fertilizer.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Keep fertilizer products away from children and pets.
  • 🚫 Do not mix different products unless the labels allow it.
  • 🌱 Natural products can still cause problems when overused.
  • 💡 If unsure, choose a simple option and follow reliable product or gardening guidance.
