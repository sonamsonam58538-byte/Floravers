GROWING BASICS

Everything Your Flowers Need

Build a strong foundation by understanding the environment around your plants.

🌱 Planting Guide

Good planting starts with the right location, healthy soil and enough space for roots to develop.

  • Choose a suitable location.
  • Use soil with good drainage.
  • Give plants enough growing space.
  • Plant according to the flower's requirements.

☀️ Understanding Sunlight

Light affects growth, leaf development and flowering. Different flowers prefer different levels of sunlight.

  • Observe how much direct light your space receives.
  • Place sun-loving plants in brighter locations.
  • Protect shade-loving flowers from harsh exposure.
  • Watch plants for signs of light stress.

💧 Watering Flowers

Watering should depend on the plant, soil and weather rather than following one fixed schedule.

  • Check the soil before watering.
  • Avoid constantly soggy soil.
  • Containers may dry faster than garden beds.
  • Adjust watering during hot or rainy periods.

🌍 Soil & Drainage

Healthy roots need both moisture and access to air. Good drainage helps prevent waterlogged soil.

  • Use suitable soil for the flower.
  • Make sure excess water can escape.
  • Avoid compacted soil.
  • Refresh container soil when needed.

🌾 Fertilizer & Nutrition

Plants use nutrients for leaves, roots and flowers. Feeding should always match the plant's needs.

  • Follow the product label when using fertilizer.
  • Avoid overfeeding plants.
  • Healthy soil supports long-term growth.
  • Consider the plant's growth stage.

✂️ Flower Care

Regular observation and simple maintenance can keep flowers looking healthy throughout their growing season.

  • Remove damaged plant material carefully.
  • Keep growing areas clean.
  • Support tall or climbing plants when needed.
  • Observe plants regularly.

SEASONAL CARE

Care Through the Seasons

Growing conditions change throughout the year, so plant care should change with them.

🌷 Spring

Spring is a common growing period for many flowers. Focus on fresh growth, suitable planting conditions and gradually increasing care as plants develop.

☀️ Summer

Monitor soil moisture and heat exposure. Containers can dry more quickly during warm weather.

🍂 Autumn

As temperatures change, observe growth and adjust watering and placement according to the plant.

❄️ Winter

Many plants grow more slowly during colder periods. Avoid treating winter plants exactly like summer plants.

PLANT PROBLEMS

Common Growing Problems

Learn what to observe when your plant does not look healthy.

🍃 Yellow Leaves

Check watering, light, drainage and the overall condition of the plant before making changes.

🌱 Slow Growth

Review sunlight, soil condition, watering and available growing space.

🌸 Few Flowers

Flowering can depend on light, plant maturity, season, nutrition and general plant health.

🐛 Pest Signs

Inspect leaves and stems regularly. Separate affected plants when appropriate and use only suitable treatments according to their labels.

BETTER BLOOMS

Helping Flowers Bloom

Healthy flowering starts with understanding the needs of the particular plant rather than using one method for every flower.

☀️ Give Suitable Light

Make sure the flower receives the amount of light it naturally prefers.

💧 Maintain Balanced Watering

Avoid both prolonged dryness and constantly waterlogged soil.

🌍 Support Healthy Roots

Good drainage and suitable soil help create a better environment for root development.

🌸 Be Patient

Flowering depends on the plant species, season, maturity and growing conditions.
